Safety

STEAR: State of Texas Emergency Assistance Registry

A free registry that gives local emergency planners and responders information about people with disabilities, medical needs, or other access and functional needs. Sign up at stear.texas.gov or by calling 2-1-1. Registering does not guarantee a specific service in an emergency.

Register with STEAR
Travel

TSA Cares for Travelers

Traveling with a child with autism or a disability? TSA Cares can explain what to expect at security. Contact them at least 72 hours before you fly.

1-855-787-2227
Federal Relay: 711
Mon–Fri 8am–11pm ET
Weekends & holidays 9am–8pm ET

Grant

My Gym Foundation Gifts

Gifts of up to $500 in items and services, such as therapy, classes, assistive devices, medical equipment and sensory items, for children under 18 with disabilities, chronic illness, or financial hardship. Gifts are purchased directly, not given as cash.

Kids’ Waivers
